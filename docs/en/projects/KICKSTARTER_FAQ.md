# ThemisDB Kickstarter — FAQ

## 1) What is ThemisDB in simple terms?

ThemisDB is a multi-model database platform designed to bring relational, graph, vector, document, geospatial, and time-series capabilities together, with a strong focus on production use and native AI integration direction.

## 2) Why run a Kickstarter for this project?

Kickstarter funding is intended to accelerate production hardening, roadmap execution, and high-quality documentation/onboarding so more teams can adopt ThemisDB with lower risk and faster time-to-value.

## 3) Is this just an idea, or is there already real work done?

There is already substantial engineering and documentation work in place. The campaign is focused on acceleration and productization, not starting from zero.

## 4) Who is ThemisDB for?

It is for teams that want to reduce complexity from fragmented data stacks, while keeping strong technical standards for security, operations, and reliability.

## 5) How is ThemisDB different from using many separate databases and AI tools?

ThemisDB’s approach is integration-first: one platform surface, one governance direction, and one roadmap, instead of maintaining many disconnected systems with separate operational overhead.

## 6) What will campaign funds be used for?

Primary uses include:

- production hardening and reliability improvements,
- roadmap milestone acceleration,
- and stronger developer/operator documentation and onboarding.

## 7) What are the biggest project risks?

Main risks are scope complexity, integration edge cases, and balancing speed with stability. We address this through milestone-based delivery, explicit priorities, and transparent progress reporting.

## 8) How will backers know progress is real?

We plan to communicate progress via clear milestone updates with concrete completion status and visible deliverables, rather than vague status language.

## 9) Will ThemisDB remain transparent in its technical direction?

Yes. Transparency in roadmap, documentation, and delivery status is a core part of how trust is built with both users and backers.

## 10) What does success look like after the campaign?

Success means backers can clearly see a stronger production-readiness baseline, faster onboarding quality, and measurable delivery progress on core priorities.
