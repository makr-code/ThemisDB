## Executive Summary

ThemisDB addresses a **$52.3B total addressable market** (TAM) in multi-model databases, vector search, and AI/ML infrastructure. With a serviceable obtainable market (SOM) of **$471M-$942M** by 2030, ThemisDB can achieve **$760M ARR** at 4.83% market share across three business segments: Community (open source), Enterprise (self-hosted), and Hyperscaler (managed service).

**Key Projections (2030):**
- **Total ARR:** $760M  
- **Market Share:** 4.83% (within 3-6% target)
- **Customers:** 2,800+ (1,000 Enterprise + 1,800 Hyperscaler)
- **Profitability:** $697M annual profit (92% margin)
- **5-Year Cumulative Profit:** $1.22B on $126.5M investment

**Investment Thesis:**
- Profitable from **Year 1** ($9.1M profit on $12.6M revenue)
- **966% ROI** over 5 years
- Unique market position (only open-source multi-model with Spanner-like sharding)
- Strong competitive moats (TrueTime, Raft consensus, AI/ML native)

## Competitive Strategy

### Market Share Capture

**From PostgreSQL/MySQL (30% of growth):**
- Migrate complex workloads needing multi-model
- AI/ML use cases (vector search)
- Better horizontal scaling story

**From MongoDB (25% of growth):**
- Superior sharding (Raft + TrueTime vs basic sharding)
- Multi-model advantage (6 vs 2 models)
- Better AI/ML integration

**From Pinecone/Weaviate (20% of growth):**
- True multi-model (not just vectors)
- Self-hosted option (Pinecone is cloud-only)
- Lower total cost (bundled features)

**From AWS/GCP/Azure (25% of growth):**
- Cost savings (30-46%)
- No vendor lock-in
- Multi-cloud portability
