Cron Syntax Supported:
  • Wildcards: * (any value)
  • Ranges: 0-5 (values from 0 to 5)
  • Lists: 1,3,5 (specific values)
  • Steps: */15 (every 15 units)
  • Step Ranges: 0-30/5 (every 5 from 0 to 30)

Example Expressions:
  "0 9-17 * * 1-5"     → Weekdays 9-17h (from issue)
  "*/15 * * * *"       → Every 15 minutes
  "0 2 * * *"          → Daily at 2 AM
  "0 0 1 * *"          → First day of month
  "30 3 * * 0"         → Sundays at 3:30 AM
