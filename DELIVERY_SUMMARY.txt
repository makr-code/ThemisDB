   Before: std::string value;

4. query_plan_visualizer.cpp Line 197 - Text Output
   Before: std::string out;

   Before: No pre-allocation
   After:  nodes_out.reserve(8192); edges_out.reserve(4096); dot.reserve(12288);
   Impact: Graph rendering +2-3x, especially for large plans (50+ nodes)
