SCREENSHOT 3: Graph Relationships
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Command: From 3_graph_relationships.aql
  MATCH (c:Customer)-[:PLACED]->(o:Order)-[:CONTAINS]->(p:Product)
  WHERE c.country IN ['DE', 'AT']
  RETURN c.name, COUNT(o.order_id) AS order_count,
         SUM(o.total_amount) AS total_spent,
         COLLECT(DISTINCT p.category) AS categories

What to capture:
  - Customer names (Alice Johnson, Bob Schmidt, etc.)
  - order_count (2, 1, 1, etc.)
  - total_spent (699.98, 4999.00, etc.)
  - categories as list

Optional: If you can show graph visualization:
  - Visual graph with nodes (Customer, Order, Product)
  - Edges showing relationships

Filename: 03-graph-relationships.png
