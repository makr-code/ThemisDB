[SCENE 4: GRAPH QUERIES - 2:30 to 3:30] (1 minute)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
File: demo/queries/3_graph_relationships.aql

Command:
  MATCH (c:Customer)-[:PLACED]->(o:Order)-[:CONTAINS]->(p:Product)
  WHERE c.country IN ['DE', 'AT']
  RETURN c.name, COUNT(o.order_id) AS order_count,
         SUM(o.total_amount) AS total_spent,
         COLLECT(DISTINCT p.category) AS categories
  GROUP BY c.customer_id
  ORDER BY total_spent DESC;

What to do:
  1. Paste the graph query
  2. Press ENTER
  3. Show the relationship results

What to say:
