================================================================================
KNOWN LIMITATIONS
================================================================================

1. Regex-Based Detection
   - Not full C++ AST analysis
   - May have false positives in complex templates
   - Macro expansions not analyzed

2. Scope Analysis
   - Uses brace counting (not semantic scoping)
   - Cannot distinguish all scope boundaries
   - Lambda captures not tracked

3. Pattern Specificity
   - Detects specified patterns
   - Subtle variations may be missed
   - Idioms not explicitly covered

Note: These are intentional trade-offs for performance and simplicity.
The detection patterns are conservative with high confidence in accuracy.
